# Archived workflow diagrams

The diagrams and their generators in this folder describe the retired
code-and-content workflow. They are kept as design history, including the
manually edited draw.io document. They are not instructions for a page run.

The active process is `docs/agents/page-workflow.md`, invoked through
`$page-draft` in ChatGPT/Codex or `/page-draft` in Claude Code. It writes
content directly to Sanity and records decisions on the Basecamp card.
