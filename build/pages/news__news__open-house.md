**CMS state: Unpublished** (not visible on the live site)

On April 22nd between 2 and 4 PM, join Camp Setebaid for a camp open house! Camp Mount Luther staff will be offering tours of the campground, while Camp Setebaid staff will be available to answer questions about our programming. This is a great opportunity to visit the campsite and develop a feeling for the life-changing experience of diabetes camp. Please join us by RSVP'ing at [info@setebaidservices.org](mailto:info@setebaidservices.org) or by calling (570) 524-9090. We hope to see you there!
