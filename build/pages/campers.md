## A Note About Vaccinations

As camps which serve youth living with diabetes, Setebaid Services follows the American Academy of Pediatrics’ recommendation for vaccinations. To attend camp, **ALL vaccinations** recommended by the US CDC (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention) **must be up-to-date**.
