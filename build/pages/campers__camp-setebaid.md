### Camp Setebaid® at Mount Luther

Exclusively for youth with diabetes ages 7\*-14, Camp Setebaid at Mount Luther offers age-specific summer camp programs which allow campers to feel at ease and accepted in a community where having **diabetes is the norm**, not the exception.

Camp Setebaid at Mount Luther is held in Mifflinburg, Pennsylvania, just 45 minutes East of State College and a little over an hour and a half Northeast of Altoona.

This summer will prove to be an incredible experience for Camp Setebaid campers!

Exciting **outdoor activities**, **lifetime memories**, **new friendship**s, and **independence** away from home make Camp Setebaid at Mount Luther the summer fun all children want to return to.

Exclusively for youth with diabetes ages 7\*-14, Camp Setebaid at Mount Luther offers **age-specific programs** that allow campers to feel at ease and accepted in a community where having **diabetes is the norm**, not the exception.

Under skilled medical supervision, campers also learn to better **understand diabete**s and the process of **self-management** by checking their **blood-glucose levels, administering insulin, and recording results**.

As campers participate in daily events, they will gain **new confidence** in managing diabetes while maintaining an active lifestyle.

But don’t worry, we have no classes at Camp Setebaid! Everything you learn, you pick up from the daily activities.

The one similarity…**every program is FUN**!

Specific activities at Camp Setebaid at Mount Luther include:

- Archery
- Arts & Crafts
- Cabin Wars
- Campfires & Camp Songs
- Canoeing
- Court Games: Basketball, and Gaga
- Field Games: Soccer, Baseball
- Frisbee Golf
- Hiking
- Nature Lore
- Low Ropes Course
- Swimming (in pool)
- Volleyball

7 year-old campers must be able to check their own blood sugar & have their doctor confirm their ability. Please contact the camp office for more information.
