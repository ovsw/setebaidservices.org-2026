On April 28th between 5 and 6 PM, join HDYC & Camp Setebaid for an open house! Staff will be available to answer questions and give tours of the campground. This is a great opportunity to visit the campsite and develop a feeling for the life-changing experience of diabetes camp. Please join us by RSVP'ing at [info@setebaidservices.org](mailto:info@setebaidservices.org) or by calling (570) 524-9090. We hope to see you there!
