ExtraGive is TODAY, and we are asking you to join us in our efforts to make this the most awesome day of giving your community has ever seen!

We are writing to ask you to take five minutes to give to [Camp Setebaid](https://www.extragive.org/organizations/setebaidservices). After that, you can give to other nonprofits that do work that you support or just kick back and watch how high the giving can go at extragive.org!

Your donation of any amount will help support campers in need. $50 provides one camper breakfast for a week. $75 provides lunch for one camper for a week. $150 provides one week of overnight lodging for a camper.

Here’s how you can make a HUGE difference today:

- Make a [donation](https://www.extragive.org/organizations/setebaidservices).
- Spread the word. Text a friend. Forward this email. Post on Facebook, Twitter and Instagram using the hashtag ExtraGive. Oh, and you can also just tell someone in person – that works, too.
