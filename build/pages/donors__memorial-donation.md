# Memorial Donation

*Create a Living Memorial*

Old URL: https://setebaidservices.org/donors/memorial-donation/

You can create a **living memorial** in the name of a family member or friend.

Your **tax-deductible donation** will show the family of the deceased **you care** about their loved one while helping people with living with diabetes through one of Setebaid Services’® summer camps or programs.

**We'll mail a card** personalized with your message to the person you designate to notify of your donation (the amount of the gift will not be indicated).

If you prefer, you can make your donation by **mail**. Simply mail us the donation and this completed [Memorial Form](https://setebaidservices.org/site/assets/files/1064/memorial_gift_form_2016.pdf).

To make your donation online, first, fill out the Memorial Contribution Form (below) and then, when prompted, complete the donation [here](https://setebaidservices.networkforgood.com/projects/61665-memorial-contributions) and choose "Memorial Contribution" as how you wish to direct the donation.

You will receive a receipt immediately. Your Memorial Card will be processed during our next business day (Monday-Friday except holidays). If there are any questions, we will contact you via email or phone provided by you.

**Thank you** for supporting our diabetes-related programs.
