# Events

### Join us to and show your support for Camp Setebaid and the Harrisburg Diabetic Youth Camp:

## Dahlias for Diabetes - You Give. We Give. August 14-22, 2026

[Dahlias for Diabetes](http://dahliasfordiabetes.com) is back! In 2025, Lilly Huber and her Mom, LeeAnn, raised approximately $1,600 for our not-for-profit organization, Setebaid Services, by selling fresh-cut flowers. This year, they're blooming again with the goal of raising $3,000 to support our mission to "turn diabetes around" and make a difference for children living with Type 1 Diabetes (T1D). From Aug 14 - Aug 22, your $20+ donation will be matched with a donated dahlia bouquet sent to a nonprofit in their area, spreading joy while helping youth living with T1D. Donors will receive a fundraiser update sharing where all the bouquets were delivered.

## Dueling Pianos with the Flying Ivories October 16, 2026, from 7-10 pm Fero Vinyards & Winery

Join us for a fun evening of interactive entertainment that blends music, comedy, and audience participation!

The event features [The Flying Ivories](https://www.flyingivories.com/) in the [Fero Vineyards & Winery](https://www.ferovineyards.com/) pavilion. Doors will open at 6 PM, with a meal provided by [Catering for You](https://cateringforyou.co/) at 6:15. Fero's fabulous selection of wines will be available for purchase all evening.

[Tickets](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/1998799873227?aff=oddtdtcreator) are $60 per person or reserve a table that seats 8 for $400. All tickets include the meal. **Must be 21 years old or older to attend. ID required.**

Song requests will be available for a donation. There will also be a basket raffle.

All proceeds from this fundraiser help support the Setebaid Services, Inc., Campership Fund for Camp Setebaid and the Harrisburg Diabetic Youth Camp. Limited seating is available. For more information call 570-524-9090 or email info@setebaidservices.org.
