**Camper Fees:**

**Every child with diabetes may receive a partial scholarship from the Setebaid Campership Fund.**

Setebaid Services uses a tiered pricing system to share the true cost of operating diabetes camp. The tiered pricing system is an “honor system” designed to make camp more affordable and accessible. Tier I is the true cost for our not-for-profit organization to operate the camp. Tiers II & III are below the true cost of camp, so we ask all parents who can afford to pay Tier I to please do so. All chosen Tiers are confidential and all children receive the same great camp experience regardless of what their family paid.

For families who need additional support, we offer Tier IV, the “Sliding Scale Fee Application” or “Financial Assistance Application” programs. Tier IV programs are income-based programs which **assure camp is available for every child living with diabetes.**

**Why Do Families Need to Apply for Tier IV?**

The Sliding Scale Fee and Financial Assistance programs are in place to assure our donors we are appropriate stewards of their donations. The programs were developed to assure our donors their gifts are shared equitably with families most in-need. **Our Campership Fund May Be Able To Assist You. [Click Here](https://setebaidservices.org/parents/campership-fund/) For More Information.**
