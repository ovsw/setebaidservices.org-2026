# World Diabetes Day

Old URL: https://setebaidservices.org/news/news/national-diabetes-awareness-month/

**CMS state: Unpublished** (not visible on the live site)

Posted: 2016-11-01

November is National Diabetes Awareness Month, and November 14th is World Diabetes Day. Unfortunately, there are still people who do not understand the impact this disease has on an individual. During the month of November, the diabetes community must come together to continue to inform the masses on what diabetes is, the importance of funding to create new diabetes management products, and ultimately, a cure.

During November, please feel free to use these cover photos for Facebook & Twitter to inform others of National Diabetes Awareness Month and World Diabetes Day.
