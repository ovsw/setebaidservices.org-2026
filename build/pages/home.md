## Welcome to Setebaid Services!

## Diabetes Education & Support at Summer Camp

Setebaid Services® is a not-for-profit organization providing diabetes education and support **summer camp** programs for **children and teens with diabetes** and their families.

Our primary goal is to enhance the quality of life for youth living with diabetes and their families, and provide them with **enjoyable summer camp experiences** which are shared with their peers.

Our programs aim to promote a **healthy lifestyle** while fostering a community of support from families who face similar challenges on a daily basis.

**[Request Info](https://setebaidservices.org/contact-us/request-info/)**

**Fun, Unique & Educational Diabetes Summer Camps:**

Several **diabetes summer camps** take a fun, unique, and hands-on approach to diabetes education in a support group setting. The education is intentional and integrated so well into the programs that **the participants don’t realize they are learning**!

Those who attend our summer camps return home with a new sense of **independence** and **understanding**.

**The bonus**: They have gained new friends who share living with diabetes and understand their life’s challenges!

**We look forward to seeing you soon at camp!**
