Diabetes summer camp is a **life-changing experience** for a child with Type 1 Diabetes (T1D).

**Be a Hero** for children living with T1D by supporting the Setebaid Walk!

Your gifts will truly impact the lives of children & teens living with Type 1 Diabetes.

This year, the walk will be on **September 16th** at **[Knoebels Amusement Resort](http://www.knoebels.com/)**. It will be held before Family Day.

Walk registration begins at **9:30 AM**, and the walk takes place at **10:00 AM**.

Support a team, or create your own by visiting **[setebaidwalk.org](http://setebaidwalk.org/).**The walk website will be live the 2nd week of August.
