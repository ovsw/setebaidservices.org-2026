## Reunite With Your Camp Setebaid Family for a Hawaiian Themed Party at Knoebels!

### We’ve missed each other long enough! **Join your Camp Setebaid friends** at **Family Day at Knoebels Amusement Resort** where we’ll engage in beloved camp activities. We’ll sing fun camp songs, play games, and enjoy camp comradery.

### Come catch up with your camp friends for a shared meal, complete with **CARB COUNTS**! Visit the **camp store** and peruse the camp photo albums.

Enjoy the awesome basket raffles and a 50/50 drawing which will help support the Setebaid Campership Fund.

### Families, campers, friends, alumni, volunteers, and future campers are invited to join us. Registration includes all activities at Pavilion U, a meal, and your 2021 Camp Setebaid T-Shirt\*. *(\*Not available for children 4 and under. Only those registered before June 30, 2021 are guaranteed a T-Shirt)*. This is a family program and we encourage all family members to attend; at least one adult (age 18 or over) must register, be present, and be responsible for each family.

### **Family Day** will take place on **July 31, 2021** beginning at **10:30 AM**. Our meal will be served at 11 AM.

Knoebels Amusement Resort is located at 391 Knoebels Boulevard, Route 487, in Elysburg, Pennsylvania 17824.

### **Registration is just $25** per person (children 4 years and under are free). **[Click here to Register.](https://setebaid.campbrainregistration.com/) Registration closes at 12 Noon on July 20, 2021**, so don't delay!

Discount ride tickets may be purchased during registration if you’d like to go into the Amusement Park.

New to Knoebels? Check out everything the #1 Family Fun Park has to offer by clicking [here](http://www.knoebels.com/).
