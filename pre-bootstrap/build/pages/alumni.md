# Alumni

*Setebaid Alumni*

Old URL: https://setebaidservices.org/alumni/

Alumni are anyone (e.g. campers, staff, or participants) who have attended one of our summer camps or programs:

- The Badger Diabetes Youth Camp in Spencer, NY
- Camp Needles in the Pines in Blounts Creek, NC
- Camp Setebaid® held at Camp Louise in Shickshinny, PA
- Camp Setebaid® at Mount Luther in Mifflinburg, PA
- Camp Setebaid® at Swatara in Bethel, PA
- The Diabetes Family Weekend in Danville, PA
- Camp Setebaid® at Camp Victory in Millville, PA
- The Diabetes Family Weekend at Split Rock Resort in Lake Harmony, PA
- The Harrisburg Diabetic Youth Camp in Millville, PA or Bethel, PA

Attending a Setebaid camp makes you a **part of our family**!

Setebaid alumni **continue to meet** even after camp is over. **Events** are held throughout the year to keep the connections you make at our programs strong. Many of our alumni even **return as counselors**!

And…**remember to connect with us** on social media like Facebook and Twitter!

### Give the Gift of Setebaid so Others May Benefit Like You Did!
