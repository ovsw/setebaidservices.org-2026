Proceeds from this tournament fund Camp Setebaid®, a diabetes camp for children living with Type 1 Diabetes (T1D). Camp Setebaid, which serves over 250 children living with T1D annually, teaches them to check their blood sugar, inject insulin, and independently manage their diabetes. Camp Setebaid has been shown to reduce families diabetes-related stress and transform the campers’ lives. Because of this transformation, Setebaid campers say it turns their diabetes around — so they named the camp “Setebaid”.
