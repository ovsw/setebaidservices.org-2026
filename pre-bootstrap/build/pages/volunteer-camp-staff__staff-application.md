Thank you for showing your interest in attending camp! Here are some quick instructions:

New Camp Staff Members: If you have already submitted the **[Staff Interest Form](https://setebaidservices.org/volunteer-camp-staff/staff-interest-form/)**, you can create an account & start filling out your information! We will contact you via email about receiving your clearances.

Returning Camp Staff Members: Thanks for returning to camp! Please submit a **[Staff Interest Form](https://setebaidservices.org/volunteer-camp-staff/staff-interest-form/)** & login to your online account. If you need help with your username or password, please call us at (570) 524-9090 or [email us](mailto:volunteer@setebaidservices.org).
