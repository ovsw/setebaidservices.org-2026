## Diabetes is a Tough Enemy, but With Summer Camp it can be Conquered!

Help **support children and teens** attend life-changing camp!

**Summer Camp** allows campers to:

- **Meet** other campers with diabetes
- **Cope** with daily struggles of managing diabetes
- **Stay safe** at camp with 24/7 on-site medical staff. Past partners included:
 - The Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia (CHOP)
 - Geisinger Department of Pediatric Endocrinology
 - The Hospital of the University of Pennsylvania
 - Lancaster General Health Family Practice Program
 - Marywood University Department of Nutrition and Dietetics
 - Neumann University Nurse Practitioner Program
 - Penn State Hershey Children’s Hospital Pediatric Endocrinology
 - The University of Pennsylvania School of Medicine
- Most importantly….**have fun**!

**Your donation**:

- Provides **scholarships** for children who could not afford camp
- Provides a **fun and educational experience** that will improve campers’ quality of life
- May be **tax-deductible**
