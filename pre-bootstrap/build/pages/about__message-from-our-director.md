# Message from our Director

*Welcome to Setebaid Services!*

Old URL: https://setebaidservices.org/about/message-from-our-director/

We’re truly delighted you’re considering becoming part of the Setebaid Family. Since our founding, we’ve been committed to empowering youth living with diabetes and their families through education, support, and an extraordinary camp experience.

Setebaid’s flagship summer camp, **Camp Setebaid®**, began in 1978 through the collaboration of a pediatric endocrinologist, a dietitian, and a teacher. Together, they created a unique model where medical knowledge, nutrition, and education blended seamlessly to provide one of the best diabetes education programs available.

In 1998, Setebaid Services® was incorporated to carry this mission forward and expand it. That same year, the **Harrisburg Diabetic Youth Camp**—founded in 1978—joined our growing family, further enriching our network and legacy.

Our philosophy is simple but powerful; we use a **team approach to teaching about Diabetes Care.** Today, we continue to use this model to teach children and teens how to manage their diabetes. Backed by university research and—most importantly—stories from campers and their families, we know our programs don’t just educate; they **transform lives.**

Our dedicated faculty come from top universities and training facilities. They serve as expert mentors to campers while also training future healthcare professionals—doctors, nurse practitioners, nurses, and dietitians alike. And remarkably, our entire team is made up of volunteers—people who are not only deeply passionate about what they do but also living examples of thriving with diabetes.

What makes Setebaid truly special is the sense of **community and family** we foster. Campers learn from our team, and our team learns just as much from them. Together, we laugh, grow, support one another, and build memories that last a lifetime.

We hope you’ll join us on this journey—to educate, to support, and to celebrate life while living with diabetes. At Setebaid Services, we’re not just a program—we’re a family.

Hoping to see you soon…

Mark Moyer, MBA, MHA
Executive Director
Setebaid Services, Inc.
