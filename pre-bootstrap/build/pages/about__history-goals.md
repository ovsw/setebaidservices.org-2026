## The Setebaid® Summer Camp Approach

Our summer camp programs take a fun, unique, and hands-on approach to diabetes education.

The “home away from home” element of our camps facilitates valuable **real-life learning experiences**.

Those who attend our programs return home with a sense of **independence** and **understanding**, and **new friends who share their same life experiences**!

## Philosophy

Setebaid Services believes diversity, equity, and inclusion are at the heart of a thriving organization. As a social services agency building a community to support those affected by diabetes, we implore everyone in our community to share in this philosophy. We empower positive change in the world when everyone in our community from all backgrounds, beliefs, identities, and abilities, are valued and actively engaged in Setebaid camps and programs. Together, we endeavor to create an expansive and inclusive community open to diversity of ideas, experiences, perspectives, and voices. By respecting others beliefs, we share the love which is required to make our camps, programs, and community prosperous.

In addition, Setebaid Services is committed to the health, safety, and welfare of its campers and staff. We take seriously our responsibility to manage risks and continually analyze campers’ well-being and safety, without compromising the purpose of any camping program: to experience the out-of-doors, to build community, to develop camper skills, to have an enjoyable recreational experience, and to foster and develop friendships. The organization understands hands-on education is an effective teaching style.

## History & Mission

Setebaid Services® was named by our beloved campers! Believing together we can “**turn diabetes around**,” our name is diabetes spelled backwards. Our programs began in 1977 and Setebaid Services was officially incorporated in 1998.

## Making a Difference for Children living with Type 1 Diabetes

Since our beginning, we have made it our mission to **make a difference** in each of our camper’s lives.

We have grown tremendously over the years and continue to do so with the help of our campers and their families, alumni, health professional partners, and donors.

We strive to show children living with diabetes they can lead **normal, healthy lives**. For one week during the summer, they are surrounded by other campers their age who face the same daily struggles of managing diabetes.

In addition, many of the Camp Counselors and other **camp staff also have diabetes**!

Our goal is to provide a **safe and fun atmosphere** for children of all ages while teaching them **healthy habits** as they manage their diabetes.

We operate in a **family atmosphere** and consistently welcome new members to our Setebaid Family.

### Forging Partnerships with Diabetes Educators

Setebaid Services’ objective is to forge partnerships with healthcare providers and diabetes educators to operate diabetes summer camps for their pediatric patients with diabetes. The organization focuses on the individuals, **children and teens living with diabetes**, and recognizes education is the key to improved health for them.

This **prevention model** is a catalyst to learning, infusing medical professionals’ expertise, with certified diabetes educators and professional teachers’ educational psychology to improve how we teach youth to manage their diabetes.

Setebaid Services programs serve as the method to teach the youth; our volunteers remain key to improving the lives of the children and families we serve.

### Mission

Setebaid Services is a 501(c)(3) not-for-profit organization to enhance the quality of life for people with diabetes and their families by:

1. Educating people with diabetes and their families to accept diabetes and maintain good glycemic control while living normal, healthy lives.
2. Educating healthcare professionals and training future healthcare professionals to effectively manage and treat people with diabetes.
3. Encouraging diabetes research.

Our **diabetes summer camps for children and teens** meet our mission by educating campers and their families effective ways to manage diabetes while having fun.

- The diabetes management skills learned are more effective because they are “**caught not taught**”.
- We do not lecture campers or their families, but rather use “**teachable moments**” throughout the day where we can coach individuals as they manage their own care.

## Summer Camp Goals

Setebaid Services’ summer camps are transformational by…

- Enabling children with diabetes to meet others living with diabetes
- Easing parents’ worries associated with raising a child living with diabetes
- Providing a healthy, enjoyable camp experience for children with diabetes
- Fostering independence by increasing diabetes management skills
- Teaching children living with diabetes is not a restrictive lifestyle
- Enabling children to interact with healthcare professionals in a non-clinical setting
- Training future healthcare professionals effective diabetes management skills in a practical and effective hands-on learning environment
