## 2021 Camp Setebaid® at Swatara is one-week of adventure for COVID-19 Vaccinated Teens Living with diabetes!

## Make memories while you share **fun** and**diabetes education** with friends at Camp Setebaid at Swatara.

### **Dates: July 18-24, 2021 -**[Click Here to Register!](https://setebaid.campbrainregistration.com/)

### **Prior to registering**, please see our [COVID-19 Mitigation and Operations Plan here](https://setebaidservices.org/site/assets/files/2255/setebaid_services_covid-19_mitigation_and_operations_plan_06-15-2021.pdf).

#### Summer campers can participate in **endless activity choices**!

- Explore nature trails.
- Have fun at arts & crafts.
- Meet new friends boating or playing basketball.
- Try your hand at archery and make a bulls-eye.

#### **Opportunities for adventure** are many because we are a camp for teens who just happen to have diabetes. You’ll explore many new adventures with your friends at camp. You’ll also learn a lot about caring for your diabetes—it just comes naturally at camp, because everyone has diabetes.

#### Under supervision of medical professionals, campers learn to:

1. **check their blood-glucose levels**
2. **administer insulin,** and
3. **record results**.

By learning **diabetes management**, campers will build a sense of **competence**and take home **new skills** to enrich their everyday lives.

But don’t worry, we have no diabetes classes at Camp Setebaid! Everything you learn, you pick up from the daily activities; in other words, what you learn is caught, not taught.
