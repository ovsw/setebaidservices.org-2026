# What to Bring to Camp

*What to Bring to Summer Camp*

Old URL: https://setebaidservices.org/campers/camp-setebaid/what-to-bring-to-camp/

## What to Pack for Your Child for Camp

Clothing (Anticipate changing 2x/day)

- Socks (plenty!)
- Undergarments
- T-Shirts / Tank Tops
- Athletic shorts
- 1-2 pairs of jeans/leggings/long pants
- 1-2 Sweatshirts
- Dance appropriate outfit (dress, skirt, dress wear)
- Pajamas
- Poncho / Rain Jacket
- Bathing Suits
- Shoes (sneakers - bring one pair you can get dirty/wet) and one better pair, shower/pool shoes)
- Themed Outfits (list of theme days will be given)

Toiletries

- Towel
- Toothbrush / Toothpaste / Floss / Mouthwash
- Shower Essentials (bodywash, shampoo, conditioner, etc.)
- Face Moisturizer/ Lotion
- Hairbrush / Hair Ties
- Sunscreen / Bug Spray
- Deodorant / Perfume
- Hand Sanitizer / Wet Wipes

Miscellaneous

- Backpack
- Pens / Pencils
- Umbrella
- Watch
- Notebook
- Headlamp
- Water Bottle
- Sunglasses
- Bedding (sheet, blanket, pillow- if you did not pre-order linens)
- Hats
- Cards / Games (if you wish)

## Other Important Things to Send with Your Camper

- Water Bottle
- Writing Paper and Envelopes with Stamps
- Camera
- Flashlight and extra batteries
- Prescriptions, open insulin (open vials expire 30 days after opening)
- Pump supplies (reservoirs, infusion sets, batteries etc. We are unable to provide our campers with pump supplies)
- CGM’s and supplies - We encourage you to send your camper's CGM and ask you to send extra supplies as CGMs often need to be changed more frequently at camp. (Please note: we cannot be responsible for loss/damage to CGM systems; we do not have CGM supplies at camp)

## What NOT to Pack for Your Child for Camp

- Electronic Items (e.g. MP3 Players, Games, iPods, etc.)
- Pets
- Food
- Any type of Weapon (e.g. knives, guns-including toy guns, or anything which could be perceived to be a weapon)

Remember, Setebaid Services is not responsible for personal belongings.
