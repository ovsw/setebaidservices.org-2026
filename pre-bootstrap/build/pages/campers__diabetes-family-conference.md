# Diabetes Family Conference

Old URL: https://setebaidservices.org/campers/diabetes-family-conference/

The Diabetes Family Conference (DFC) is a full-day of programming for the entire family at lovely Camp Swatara! The DFC offers educational sessions for parents, while holding fun camp activities for children & teens with diabetes. Siblings are invited to attend!
