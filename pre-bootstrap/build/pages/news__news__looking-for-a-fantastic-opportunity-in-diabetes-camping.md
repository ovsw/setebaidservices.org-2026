# Looking for a Fantastic Opportunity in Diabetes Camping?

Old URL: https://setebaidservices.org/news/news/looking-for-a-fantastic-opportunity-in-diabetes-camping/

**CMS state: Unpublished** (not visible on the live site)

Posted: 2016-05-16

Setebaid Services, in collaboration with AmeriCorps, has constructed an exciting year-round position for those looking to make a positive impact in the lives of children living with Type 1 Diabetes. This full-time position, Volunteer Coordinator Assistant, will recruit camp staff, coordinate the camp planning work group, meet with college groups to share information about camp, train volunteers to attend diabetes fairs, and create a speakers’ bureau.

This AmeriCorps position will receive a cost-of-living stipend, AmeriCorps Health Insurance policy, and, if all hours are completed, a substantial education award. The education award may be used to pay for tuition, other approved educational expenses, or toward approved loans used for education.

We are currently interviewing for this position, which will begin in August. Please contact us immediately for more information!
