# Our Staff

*Summer Camp Leadership*

Old URL: https://setebaidservices.org/about/camp-leadership/

Every staff member who volunteers at camp fulfills an **essential role** in serving our campers with Type 1 Diabetes. Whatever responsibility they might have, camp leadership is essential in ensuring, a **safe**, **educational**, and **fun week** for our summer campers.

**Counselor:**
Counselors work **directly with campers** and ensure the campers have the **best week** of their summer.

**Senior Counselor:**
Senior Counselors have previous experience at camp and ensure the cabin **works together as a team** throughout their week at camp.

**Head Counselor:**
Head Counselors are **experienced professionals** who coach Senior Counselors, Counselors, and campers as needed.

**Counselor-In-Training Director:**
The Counselor-In-Training (CIT) Director mentors a select group of 16 & 17 year old campers through **additional camp programs** meant to build maturity & leadership.

**Camp Director:**
The Camp Director works hand-in-hand with all other camp staff members (counselors, medical team, dietitians, etc) to make sure **summer camp is a fun & safe experience for everybody.**

If you would like to **volunteer** for any of these opportunities, please fill out a “[Staff Interest Form](https://setebaidservices.org/volunteer-camp-staff/staff-interest-form/)” found under [Volunteer Camp Staff](https://setebaidservices.org/volunteer-camp-staff/)!
