## Is a Setebaid® Diabetes Summer Camp Right for You?

Setebaid summer camps are fun with **a lot of activities** every day.

From the time you wake up each morning until you fall asleep every night, **summer campers are with friends**—all who have diabetes, just like they do.

**Lifelong friendships** are formed every day at camp!

## Summer Camp Activities

At T1D Summer Camp, **no one day is the same**! There are many different activities for campers to do during camp!

Activities may include: *(Not all activities are at every camp)*

- Archery
- Arts & Crafts
- Color Wars
- Boating
- Nature Lore
- Sports & Games
- Campfires & Camp Songs
- Gaga & Volleyball
- Mini-Golf
- Ropes Course with Zip Line & Climbing Wall
- Swimming (in pool)

## Which Summer Camp Should I Choose?

Setebaid summer camps are located in Central Pennsylvania.

The major difference between the two camps is **the ages** at each camp.

Review the details at each camp to see **which camp is right for you**!

| | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CAMP** | **DATES** | **AGES** | **LOCATION** |
| The Harrisburg Diabetic Youth Camp (HDYC) | July 13-19, 2025 | 7\*-12 | Mifflinburg, PA |
| Camp Setebaid at Mount Luther | July 13-19, 2025 | 13\*\*-14 | Mifflinburg, PA |

\*7 years-old campers must be able to check their own blood sugar & have their doctor confirm their ability. Please contact the camp office for more information.

\*\* Campers must be 13 by June 1st. If at June 1st they are still 12 years-old, they may attend HDYC or Camp Setebaid at Mount Luther.
