Our campers named us. Setebaid is diabetes spelled backwards. The idea: together we can turn it around. Programs started in 1977; Setebaid Services® was incorporated in 1998.

Here, diabetes is the norm. Not the exception.

Kids first

Everyone checks blood sugar and counts carbs. Nobody is the only one.

Caught, not taught

No lectures. Staff coach in the moment, during the ordinary fun of the day.

Experts on site

Pediatric diabetes physicians, nurses and educators live at camp all week.
