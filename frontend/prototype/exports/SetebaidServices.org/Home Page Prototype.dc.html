Diabetes is the norm here, not the exception.

Kids first

Everyone at camp checks blood sugar and counts carbs. Nobody is the only one. Campers get to be kids.

Learning that doesn't feel like a lesson

Management skills are caught, not taught. Staff coach in the moment, during the ordinary fun of the day.

An expert healthcare team, all week

Physicians, nurses and educators who specialise in pediatric diabetes live on site for the whole session.
