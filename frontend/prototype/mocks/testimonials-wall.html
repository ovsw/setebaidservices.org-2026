Testimonials section: wall of love concepts

Four takes on the same goal: the viewer should feel buried in praise. All use the 32 real testimonials from the live site, the section's dark green palette, and existing type (Bricolage, Caveat, Archivo, mono eyebrows). Frames are 1320px wide, scroll sideways on small screens. Pick a letter.

A. The pile

A 4 column masonry of small cards that runs off the bottom and fades out, as if it never ends. A few cards are amber to break rhythm, a few get bigger type. Closes with a handwritten "and 1,000+ more". Feel: cork board in the camp office. Risk: cards are the one thing the rest of the page avoids, but here density makes them read as a texture, not chrome.

D. The ledger

Borrows the hairline grid the Programs and Safety sections already use, so it looks like a sibling, not a new idea. Sticky headline and proof numbers on the left, a numbered 3 column ledger of quotes on the right that fades out into an "Read all" button. Cells are numbered like entries in a guestbook (#0847...).
