01 · THE PLACE

One private island.
Zero distractions.

CAC lives on its own island in the wilderness lakes region of Temagami, Ontario. No roads in, no traffic through — just water, forest, and a camp designed entirely around kids. Phones stay home; friendships don't.

Getting there is easy — supervised bus from the Toronto area, plus airport service for international campers.
Accredited member of the Ontario Camps Association.
You're never out of the loop — daily photos and weekly videos all season long.

05 · STAFF & SAFETY

The safest place
to be brave.

First flips and first ski pulls take courage — so we surround them with a net. A mature, adult staff, a small staff-to-camper ratio with constant supervision, and a doctor who lives on the island all summer.
